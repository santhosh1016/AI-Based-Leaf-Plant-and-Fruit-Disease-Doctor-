# Problem Statement

Farmers often face difficulties in identifying plant diseases at an early stage. In many cases, they rely on manual inspection or expert advice, which may not always be available.

This leads to:
- Delay in disease detection
- Incorrect treatment
- Reduced crop quality and yield

The problem is to create a system that can help users quickly identify plant diseases using images.

The proposed solution is a mobile application that uses AI to analyze plant images and provide disease predictions along with suggestions.
